# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-05)

First public release.

- Volumetric, ray-marched smoke for solid rocket boosters: glowing exhaust jet from the measured nozzle exit, dense
  billowing column, long-lived trail that drifts, twists and spreads with the wind.
- Ground cloud at launch that shoots out of the flame trenches, towers up beside the rocket and keeps the rocket
  visible; it replaces KSP's own trench smoke.
- Self-shadowing, multiple scattering, sky and ground light, firelight; the smoke casts shadows.
- Offline preview renderer in the Unity project.
